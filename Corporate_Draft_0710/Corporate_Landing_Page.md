## 🧠 The Science of Absolute Containment (Our Foundational IP)
*(Note: The DROS-PGM execution enforcement engine is protected under U.S. Patent Application No. 64/111,973 (Patent Pending).)*

Our `364.17 ns` execution speed is not magic; it is rooted in decades of computer science research applied to a novel AI-safety domain.

### 1. Zero-Lock Deterministic Enforcement
Traditional security monitors use Mutex locks, which freeze processes and cause fatal robotic jitter. DROS-PGM utilizes a **lock-free, zero-dynamic-allocation enforcement architecture**. By separating the cryptographic attestation plane from the inline execution enforcement point, we achieve **deterministic sub-microsecond decisions**—allowing the sensor fusion pipeline to run seamlessly at millions of frames per second.

### 2. Simplex Architecture & Instant Safe-State Swapping
When DROS-PGM detects a malicious or hallucinated command from the AI, it does not attempt to "correct" the complex, unpredictable LLM. Inspired by the **Simplex Architecture** used in avionics, DROS performs an instant pointer swap to a mathematically verifiable, simple fallback controller (e.g., Drone Hovering or Arm E-Stop). The AI remains compromised, but the physical hardware remains perfectly safe.

### 3. Out-of-Band Kernel Isolation (eBPF-Inspired)
To ensure that a compromised AI agent cannot simply turn off the security monitor, DROS-PGM operates entirely outside the AI's user-space. Leveraging OS-level memory sealing and **eBPF-style telemetry interception**, the guard module is physically impossible to bypass without triggering an immediate hardware-level halt.
